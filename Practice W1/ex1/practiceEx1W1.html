Introduction to Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox video game created by Markus "Notch" Persson and developed by Mojang Studios. First released in 2009 before its full launch in 2011, it has become the best-selling video game of all time.

There are three main reasons why Minecraft became so popular:

  • Digital LEGOs Its simple "break and place blocks" mechanic gave players total creative freedom with zero forced objectives.
  • The Youtube Explosion The games emergent, open-ended nature made it perfect for "Lets Play" creators (Yogscast, PewDiePie, CaptainSparklez), creating a massive viral loop of viewers who bought the game to play along.
  • Infinite Variety via Modding & Servers: Community-made servers (like Hypixel), mini-games, and mods constantly refreshed the game with new ways to play.
